Indigenous learnings

Article 1

Indigenous people have the right to enjoyment whether in a group or alone, and are affected by human rights and all fundamental freedoms that are recognized and protected by the United Nations, Universaldeclaration of Human Rights and international human rights laws.

Article 2

Indigenous people are free and equal to all other people, they also the right as indivisuals to be free from discrimination with their rights on basis of their indigenous origins or identity.

Article 3

Indigenous people have the right to choose their political status themselves and persue economic, social and cultural development.

Article 4

Indigeonous people while using article 3 have the right to autonomy or self-governace in relation to their internal and local affairs as well as financing their autonomous actions.

This means they have the right to fund projects lead by themselves, like home buisnesses, or other buissnesses like managing of a resteraunt etc.

Article 5

Indigenous people have the right to maintain and strengthen their distinct political, legal, economic, social, cultural institutions, while still being able to properly participate in political, economic social and cultural politics.

Article 6

Indigenous people have the right of a nationality

Article 7

1. Indigenous people have the right to physical and mental integrity liberty and security of person.

2. Indigenous people as a colective have the right to live free, in peace and security as a distinct people and won't be subject to any act of genocide or other acts of vilence towards them including the removal of children

Article 8

1. Indigenous people have the right to not be forced to assimilate or lose their culture

2. States provide effective methods for the prevention or redress of:

(a) Any action that would result in the their integrity as a distinct person or of their cultural values and/or ethnic identity

(b) Any action that would remove them from their lands, territories or resources

(c) Any propaganda that promotes discrimination against them

Article 9

Indigenous peoples have the right to be part of a community or nation in accordance to the traditions and customs of community or nation and there is no discrimination cause by this action.

Article 10

Indigeonous people are not to be removed against their will by anyone, without fair trade from an Indigenous community.

Article 11

1. Indidenous people have the right to revitalise/pratice their culture this includes the right to maintain, protect and develop their culture

2. States are to provide redress for any stolen culture

Article 12

1. Indigenous people have the right to practice and alter their traditions

2. States will atempt to enable Indigenous people to access the requiredd materials for the ceremonies

Article 13

1. Indigenous people have the right to revitalize and develop their language, culture, history, oral traditions, philosophies, writing, and literature, and to be able to retain their original names

2. States are to take effect to ensure this right is protected.

Article 14

1. Indigenous people have the right to control their their education and provide education in their own language with in the laws of their culture.

2. Indigenous individuals, particularly children, have the right to education without discrimination.

3. The state will work with people to ensure to work with indigenous peoples, particularly children to have access to an education in their culture and language.

Article 15

1. Indigenous people have the right to dignity and diversity of their cultures, traditions and aspirations properly represented in education.

2. States shall consult with indigenous peoples to combat prejudice and eliminate discrimination, and promote tolerance, understanding and good ralations amoung people.

Article 16

1. Indigenous people have the right to produce media and have access to all non-indigenous media.

2. States will ensure proper representation of Indigenous peoples in media.

Article 17

1. Indigenous people have the right to be fully affected by the international and domestic labour laws.

2. States shall ensure that indigenous children are protected from economic exploitation and dangerous working conditions.

3. Indigenous people have the right to not be discriminated against in employment, salary and other terms of employment.

Article 18

Indigenous people have the right to participate in decisions that affect their rights through their own representative institutions and develop their own decision-making institutions.

Article 19

States shall consult and cooperate with indigenous peoples with good faith/intensions to get consent before implementing legistrative or administrative measure that may affect them.

Article 20

1. Indigenous people have the right to maintain and develop their political, economic and social systems.

2. Indigenous people deprived of their means of subsistence are entitled to proper redress.

Article 21

1. Indigenous people have the right to improve their economic satus without discrimination.

2. States shall ensure that indigenous people have equal access to economic opportunities.(Particular attention shall be paid to the rights and special needs of indigenous elders, women, youth, children and persons with disabilities.)
